# Lessons from previous games

## Initial lessons
- Found the first city on turn 1; waiting costs growth.
- Do not re-select the current research: it resets accumulated bulbs.

## Lessons from game 20261013-152651-hierarchy-cf1b (2026-10-13)
- Prioritize early tech research by focusing on founding cities and advancing to core technologies like Writing or Agriculture sooner.  
- Expand city placement to include key strategic locations (e.g., river crossings, trade routes) to improve early-game production and defense.  
- Build unit diversity from turn 3 onward—limit reliance on Warriors alone; add early scouts or farmers to support exploration and food production.

## Lessons from game 20261013-162725-hierarchy-97c5 (2026-10-13)
- Prioritize early tech research by focusing on foundational technologies like Farming and Woodcutting instead of delaying until later turns.  
- Avoid establishing cities too far from resource clusters; place initial cities near high-value resource zones to improve early game efficiency.  
- Establish a second city earlier—by turn 5—instead of waiting until turn 8 to reach a stable urban footprint.

## Lessons from game 20261013-163349-hierarchy-c7aa (2026-10-13)
- Prioritize early tech research by focusing on founding more cities and building unit production before turn 5 to accelerate progress.  
- Establish at least one city in a strategic location to enable faster expansion and resource gathering within the first 20 turns.  
- Avoid over-investing in early unit production without sufficient resource base; reallocate units to support city development and defense.

## Lessons from game 20261013-163505-hierarchy-0236 (2026-10-13)
- Focus on building early cities in a contiguous cluster to maximize growth and unit production within the first 50 turns.  
- Prioritize research of Bronze Working early to enable faster unit production and technological advantage.  
- Allocate early resources to expand and defend the initial city cluster to prevent early invasions or disruptions.

## Lessons from game 20261013-140210-hierarchy-eb67 (2026-10-13)
- Focus on early city development by settling adjacent plots to maximize early production and support growth.  
- Prioritize advancing tech research immediately after founding the first city to unlock essential capabilities faster.  
- Avoid overextending unit deployments in early turns—instead, use units for defense and resource gathering to ensure stability.

## Lessons from game 20261013-140521-hierarchy-e1b6 (2026-10-13)
- Prioritize early city foundation in fertile, strategic locations to maximize resource production and expansion potential.  
- Begin research earlier to unlock key technologies sooner, especially in the early turns when game pace is fastest.  
- Focus on building units for defense and exploration within the first 50 turns to secure territory and prevent early attacks.
