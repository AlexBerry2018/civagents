# Early game strategy

## Expansion first
In the first 50 turns the number of cities matters most. Found the capital on turn 1 unless the tile is clearly bad. Build Warriors first for defense, then Settlers. Each new city should be 3-5 tiles from the others, on grassland or plains, preferably near a river or special resource.

## Exploration
Send the Explorer toward unexplored areas in a consistent direction instead of moving back and forth. Enter huts on the way. Exploration reveals good city sites and neighbors.

## Workers
Keep Workers near cities. Irrigate grassland and plains next to a river, mine hills, build roads between cities. A worker that keeps wandering wastes turns.

## Science
A reliable early order: Bronze Working, Alphabet, Ceremonial Burial, Pottery, Writing, Code of Laws, Monarchy. Keep science around 60%. Do not change research every turn.

## Defense
Every city needs at least one defender, fortified inside the city. Barbarians and neighbors attack undefended cities.

## Common mistakes
Moving settlers for many turns looking for a perfect site. Leaving cities without defenders. Re-selecting research every turn. Changing government early (Anarchy).
