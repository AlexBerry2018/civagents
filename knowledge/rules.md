# Freeciv rules for the early game

## Cities
A city is founded by Settlers with the action "build city". The tile with the city always produces food, shields and trade. Cities cannot be founded right next to another city: keep new cities at least 3 tiles apart. A city grows when its food stock fills the granary; food surplus speeds growth. A city works the tiles around it, so good land nearby matters more than the city tile itself.

## Terrain
Grassland: 2 food. Plains: 1 food, 1 shield. Forest: 1 food, 2 shields. Hills: 1 food, 0 shields, strong defense, good city site with a river. Mountains: almost no food. Desert, Tundra, Swamp: poor. River on a tile adds trade and allows irrigation. Special resources (Wheat, Pheasant, Wine, Silk, Fish, Oasis, Buffalo) add extra output.

## Units
Settlers found cities and can also improve land; they cost food and population. Workers improve land: irrigation (more food on grassland/plains near water), mine (more shields on hills), road (trade, movement). Explorers move fast and see far; use them to reveal the map and enter huts. Warriors are the cheapest defenders.

## Huts
Minor Tribe Villages (huts) give random rewards: gold, a technology, free units, sometimes barbarians. Entering huts early is usually worth it.

## Research
Each city produces bulbs from trade. Research one technology at a time. Switching or re-selecting the current research can lose accumulated bulbs, so choose once and keep it.

## Government and rates
The game starts in Despotism. Rates split trade into science, tax (gold) and luxury. Monarchy (requires Ceremonial Burial and Code of Laws) is a solid early upgrade. Changing government passes through Anarchy for a few turns, which stops research.

## Happiness
Unhappy citizens can cause civil disorder: the city produces nothing. Fixes: luxury rate, temples (Ceremonial Burial), military units in the city under Despotism or Monarchy.
