# Skill assess_threat — threats to our cities (Оценить угрозу)

Used by: military, ruler, single. Input: surroundings of our units, cities, units.
How it works: finds foreign units in the surroundings (marked "[units: player N]") and cities with no combat unit on their tile.
What to do: an undefended city needs a defender - build Warriors there or move a combat unit into it and fortify. Foreign units next to a city are a reason to fortify, not to attack with explorers.
The hint appears as "[assess_threat] ...".
